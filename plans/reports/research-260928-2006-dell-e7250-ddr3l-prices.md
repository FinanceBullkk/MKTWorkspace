## Summary

The laptop has one free slot and currently uses one Nanya 4 GB DDR3L-1600 module.
Vietnam marketplace pages did not expose reliable live prices without login, so the figures below
are buying ranges rather than verified vendor quotations.

## Findings

| Upgrade | Typical used/pulled price | Typical tested/warranty price |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 4 GB DDR3L-1600 SO-DIMM | 100,000-160,000 VND | 170,000-250,000 VND |
| 8 GB DDR3L-1600 SO-DIMM | 230,000-350,000 VND | 350,000-500,000 VND |
| Matched 2 x 8 GB kit | 480,000-650,000 VND | 700,000-950,000 VND |

## Recommendation

- Best value: add one tested 4 GB DDR3L-1600 module for no more than about 200,000 VND.
- Best long-term configuration: buy a matched 2 x 8 GB kit when the total is at or below about
  700,000 VND with return or replacement coverage.
- Required specification: DDR3L-1600, PC3L-12800S, 204-pin SO-DIMM, 1.35 V, non-ECC, unbuffered.
- Prefer the same Nanya `NT4GC64C88B1NS-DI` part for the 4 GB option, but another reputable module
  with the same specification is acceptable.

## Sources Checked

- Bing-indexed Vietnam RAM retailers: CellphoneS, Tin Hoc Ngoi Sao, MemoryZone, Phong Vu.
- Public marketplace searches for Shopee, Lazada, and Cho Tot.
- Live laptop CIM inventory for installed module, slot count, speed, and capacity limit.
